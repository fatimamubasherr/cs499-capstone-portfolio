Professional Self-Assessment

How I think about computer science now

My coursework moved me from thinking about software as isolated code toward thinking about complete systems: users, requirements, algorithms, data, security, testing, communication, and long-term maintainability. The capstone made that progression visible by requiring me to revisit earlier work and improve it with more deliberate engineering choices.

Across the program, I learned that strong technical work is rarely just about producing a correct answer. It also requires communicating decisions clearly, evaluating trade-offs, anticipating failure, and building systems that other people can understand and extend.

“The strongest version of a system is not simply the one that runs. It is the one whose decisions, structure, and limitations can be understood.”
